GAME DAY
Before kickoff:
1. Read snapshot and game scope.
2. Confirm opponent, site, date, weather, active/inactive players, elevations, medical limits, and priorities.
3. Use start_game once.

During play:
1. Simulate honestly from established conditions.
2. Use record_drive after each completed drive. Include resulting score, quarter, clock, possession, and field state in payload.live.
3. Use record_game_event immediately for injuries, turnovers, major penalties, eligibility issues, or urgent events.
4. Use upsert_team_stats and upsert_player_stats for reconciled cumulative statistics.
5. Use update_live_game for quarter, halftime, timeout, possession, or field-state reconciliation not attached to a drive.
6. Official score, clock, possession, participation, injuries, and statistics come only from reconciled data. Narrative must not silently create statistics.

Final whistle:
1. Reconcile score, drives, scoring summary, turnovers, penalties, sacks, special teams, participation, injuries, and tracked stats.
2. Mark unsupported fields NOT_TRACKED.
3. Use finalize_game once with current live-game expected_version.
4. Include next-session timeline or opponent changes in payload.state_patch.
5. Do not proceed until finalization succeeds.
